## Troubleshooting: AcmeHub Won't Connect to Wi-Fi

**Symptoms:** Hub shows red light, app says "Device Offline"

**Steps:**
1. Confirm your Wi-Fi is 2.4 GHz — AcmeHub does not support 5 GHz networks.
2. Move the hub within 10 feet of your router and retry.
3. Press the reset button on the bottom of the hub for 10 seconds until the light flashes white.
4. Open the Acme app → Add Device → Follow setup wizard.
5. If still failing: power cycle your router, then retry steps 3–4.

**Escalate if:** After 2 resets the hub still won't connect.

## Troubleshooting: AcmeSensor Not Responding

**Symptoms:** Sensor shows "No Signal" in app, automations not triggering.

**Steps:**
1. Replace the CR2032 battery — low battery is the most common cause.
2. Ensure the sensor is within 30 feet of the AcmeHub.
3. In the app: Settings → Devices → [Sensor Name] → Remove Device, then re-add.
4. Check that AcmeHub firmware is up to date (app: Hub Settings → Check for Updates).

---

## Troubleshooting: AcmeLight Flickering or Not Responding

**Symptoms:** Bulb flickers, won't turn on/off via app, or is stuck on a colour.

**Steps:**
1. Toggle the physical switch off → wait 5 seconds → toggle back on.
2. In the app, tap the light → tap the three-dot menu → Reset Light.
3. If the bulb still flickers: the dimmer switch may be incompatible. AcmeLights require a smart dimmer or standard on/off switch — not a traditional dimmer.
4. Replace the bulb if flickering persists after steps 1–3.

## Order & Shipping FAQ

**Where is my order?**
Provide the customer's order number and use the check_order_status tool.

**Standard shipping:** 3–5 business days (UPS Ground).
**Expedited shipping:** 1–2 business days (UPS 2nd Day Air).
**International:** 7–14 business days. Customs delays may apply.

**Return policy:** 30 days from delivery, product must be in original packaging.
To initiate a return: Acme app → Orders → [Order] → Return Item.

## Escalation Triggers

Always escalate to a human agent when:
- Customer reports injury or property damage.
- Issue has been reproduced 3+ times without resolution.
- Customer explicitly requests a human.
- Billing disputes or refund requests over $100.

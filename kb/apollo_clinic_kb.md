## How to Book an Appointment

The voice agent (Priya) can book appointments directly. She will ask for:
1. Your full name
2. Your concern or symptoms
3. Which doctor or specialty you prefer
4. Your preferred date and time

Appointments are 30 minutes each. Confirmation is sent via Google Calendar.

Alternatively: visit apollo247.com or call 6366530173.

## Common Questions

**Q: Do I need to visit in person for a first consultation?**
Yes, first consultations are in-person. Follow-up consultations may be available via Apollo 24|7 app.

**Q: Can I get a second opinion?**
Yes. Apollo Hospitals offers formal second opinion services. Ask Priya to connect you with the right specialist.

**Q: What documents should I bring?**
Bring a valid photo ID, any previous medical reports, prescriptions, and scan/test reports.

**Q: Is parking available?**
Yes, parking is available at the hospital premises.

**Q: Do you have a pharmacy?**
Yes, an on-site pharmacy is open during hospital hours.

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**
Cash, all major credit/debit cards, UPI (PhonePe, GPay, Paytm), and most insurance plans.

**Q: Is the hospital empanelled with insurance?**
Apollo Hospitals Navi Mumbai is empanelled with most major insurance providers. Bring your insurance card and policy number.

## Escalation Triggers

Transfer the caller to a human staff member if:
- The caller reports a medical emergency — advise them to call 108 first
- The caller is distressed or needs immediate assistance
- The caller asks about billing disputes or insurance claims
- The caller requests to speak with a doctor directly
- The tool fails to book an appointment after two attempts
